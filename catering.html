Catering

The majority of our most popular menu items are available for catering, including our cakes and cookies.

Or, if you prefer, our professional staff can come to your celebration, deliver, and even serve our happiness directly to you!
We have a variety of catering and party options to suit your needs so feel free to contact us for further information.
